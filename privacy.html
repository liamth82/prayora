How Ora handles your information

Last updated 3 November 2026

Ora is made to keep you close to prayer, not to collect data about you. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you write and keep in Ora stays on your phone.

Ora is published by Westworld PR Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company no. 14914271). We are the data controller for the small amount of personal information described below. You can reach us at hello@prayora.co.

What stays on your phone

These are stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. We never receive them:

  • Your entries in Refuge, Deo gratias and My Wisdom.
  • Your reading progress, bookmarks and reader settings.
  • Your prayer, fasting and bell settings, and the days you have marked as read or prayed.
  • Whether you have turned on the app lock. If you use a fingerprint, face or passcode lock, your phone checks it; Ora never sees or stores your biometric data.

Reminders for the Hours and the Angelus are scheduled on your phone. Read aloud uses your phone's own voice. Deleting the app deletes all of this.

Ask

When you use Ask, the conversation you are having (your questions and Ora's answers so far), together with the date and today's feast, is sent to our server so that an answer can be written from Ora's library. To write the answer, our server sends that conversation to Anthropic, the company that provides the Claude AI model.

  • We do not store your questions or answers, and Ask is not linked to your name, email or any account.
  • Anthropic processes the conversation on our behalf to produce the answer. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use it to train its models. Anthropic may keep it for a short period for safety and abuse monitoring, as its own policies describe.
  • Please don't include your name, contact details or other personal information in a question. Ask is not a priest or a counsellor, and it is not confidential in the way the confessional is.
  • Tapping New clears the conversation from your phone.

The waitlist

If you join the waitlist on prayora.co, we keep your email address, the type of phone you chose and the date you signed up. We use these only to tell you when Ora is ready and to send occasional news about it. They are stored in Airtable, which we use as our database.

Our legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by replying to any email from us or writing to hello@prayora.co, and we will delete your details. We don't sell or share the list.

App updates and our website

  • When it opens, the app checks Expo's update service for new content. That check includes basic technical details such as the app version and platform, not anything you have written.
  • prayora.co and our server are hosted by Vercel, which keeps short-lived technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts.
  • The website uses no cookies and no analytics.

Payments

If Ora offers a paid subscription, payment is handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details; we receive only the information needed to confirm your subscription.

Where your information goes

The services above (Anthropic, Airtable, Vercel and Expo) may process information in the United States. Where they do, they protect it with safeguards recognised under UK data protection law, such as standard contractual clauses.

Children

Ora is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, object to its use, or withdraw consent. Write to hello@prayora.co and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change how Ora handles information, we will update this page and the date above. For significant changes, we will also tell you in the app.
