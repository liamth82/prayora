Ora

Ora et labora. Pray, and work.

A Catholic companion for a phone that has forgotten how to be quiet. Scripture, the Hours and the saints are here. The feed is not.

Ora counts the minutes you spend in it and asks you to leave. When you want to put the phone down properly, the digital fast locks it for as long as you choose.
