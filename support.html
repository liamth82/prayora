Help with Ora

We read every message.

For help, a question, a correction to a reading or a saint, or an idea, email hello@prayora.co. We aim to reply within two working days.

If something isn't working, it helps to tell us which phone you use, which screen you were on and what happened.

Common questions

Is what I write in Refuge, Deo gratias and My Wisdom private?
Yes. It is stored only on your phone and never sent to us. If you delete the app, it is deleted too.

How does Ask work?
Ask answers only from the texts in Ora's library and shows you the source for each point, so you can read it for yourself. It is not a priest, and it cannot replace confession, spiritual direction or your parish. If you are struggling or in danger, please speak to someone now: in the UK and Ireland call Samaritans on 116 123, in the US call or text 988, or contact your local emergency services.

Which calendar does Ora follow?
You can choose the Ordinary Form or the 1962 Missal in Settings. Some feasts are moved in particular countries; if your parish keeps a feast on a different day, Ora may not show that yet.

I'm not getting the bells for the Hours.
Check that notifications for Ora are allowed in your phone's settings, and that the bells are turned on in Ora's Settings.

How do I get the latest version?
Ora checks for updates each time it opens. You can also use Check for updates in Settings.

How do I cancel a subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone, go to Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile, then Payments and subscriptions.

How do I delete my data?
Delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone. To be removed from the waitlist, email hello@prayora.co. Our privacy page explains what we hold.
